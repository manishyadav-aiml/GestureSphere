GestureSphere — Bringing Hand Gestures to Life in Real Time.

GestureSphere uses MediaPipe to track 21 landmarks per hand, recognises gestures using geometric rules, and renders interactive energy effects on a Canvas. Everything runs locally in your browser. Your webcam video is not uploaded to a server by this application.

How to Use GestureSphere

  1. Press Start Camera and allow webcam access.
  2. Hold an open palm facing the camera. The energy orb appears above your hand and charges.
  3. Close your fist to release the energy.
  4. Use both hands to control their respective visual effects.

Tip: Good lighting and keeping your whole hand in view can improve tracking.
